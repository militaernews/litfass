No ad network · no tracking · one JSON feed

What it does

📮 Your own posters

Adverts for your channels, projects and partners, managed in a Telegram bot: texts per language, link, image, schedule and weight.

🗂 Adverts that fit

Placements register themselves and pick their content (news, gaming, tech …). Their feed only carries adverts for that content.

🎨 Styled to match

Colour presets or your own colours, as a card, banner or poster. On the web it's themable with CSS variables.

📊 Honest numbers

Views, clicks and completed rewarded views as daily totals per advert and placement. No IP addresses, no profiles, no cookies.

Show adverts in your project

  1. Open @litfass_advertisement_bot and send /register.
  2. Name your site, bot or channel and pick the content you publish.
  3. Use the feed URL the bot gives you with one of the packages below. /stats shows how it's going.
// Web: bun add github:militaernews/litfass
import { createReporter, eventUrl, fetchFeed, injectAdStyles, pickAd, renderAd } from '@militaernews/litfass';

const feed = await fetchFeed(FEED_URL);
const reporter = createReporter(eventUrl(FEED_URL));
const ad = pickAd(feed, { placement: 'banner' });
if (ad) {
	injectAdStyles();
	slot.append(renderAd(ad, { onClick: (ad) => reporter.report(ad, 'click') }));
	reporter.report(ad, 'view');
}

Keep it legal

Adverts have to be recognisable as adverts (in Germany § 6 DDG and § 5a UWG), so the renderers label them „Anzeige“ by default. The feed and the statistics hold no personal data. If you load the feed straight from a visitor's browser, their IP address reaches the feed server for that request, but it isn't logged or stored. To keep even that off, fetch the feed on your own server.
